    01 — Overview

    Introduction

    Video games have become an integral part of modern culture, especially since the technological advancements of the 1980s. As demand continues to rise, analyzing market trends is essential. This project uncovers significant patterns in global video game sales data — addressing trends over time, regional genre preferences, and publisher dominance — using Excel.

    02 — Dataset

    Data Overview

    The dataset was sourced from Kaggle, containing global video game sales from the 1980s to the 2010s — 16,598 entries in total.

    03 — Preprocessing

    Data Preprocessing

    Format Conversion

    Column formats were standardized: the Year column was converted to Number, and Sales columns were treated as units sold in millions since currency was not specified. Other columns were already in a usable general format.

    Column format conversion in Excel

    Handling Missing Years

    The dataset contained blanks in the Year column. The median was calculated using =MEDIAN(Sales[Year]), which returned 2007 — used to fill all null Year values.

    Median year (2007) used to fill null Year values

    Handling Missing Publishers

    Entries with "unknown" or blank Publisher values were removed entirely — they represented only 1.57% of the dataset (261 out of 16,598 entries), making removal the cleaner choice over imputation.

    261 entries removed due to unknown Publisher values

    04 — Analysis & Findings

    Key Findings

    Sales Trend Over Time

    Global video game sales grew significantly from the late 1990s through the mid-2000s, peaking around 2008. A steady decline followed — likely driven by the shift toward digital distribution and mobile gaming, which this dataset does not capture.

    Global video game sales trend — 1980s to 2010s

    Regional Market Share

    North America was the dominant market, accounting for nearly half of all global sales. Europe followed at over a quarter of the total.

    Regional breakdown of global video game sales

    Most Popular Genres

    Action (3,281 games) and Sports (2,317 games) dominated by volume and were consistently the top sellers across all regions. Notable titles include GTA and Assassin's Creed (Action), and FIFA and Wii Sports (Sports).

    Japan is the exception. Role-Playing games account for nearly 30% of Japan's sales — versus only 8–9% in other regions. Pokémon and Final Fantasy drive this gap significantly.

    Action and Sports genre volume

    05 — Insights

    What the Data Suggests

    NA and EU are the biggest prizes. Publishers targeting these markets should prioritize Action and Sports titles — the genres with the strongest proven demand in both regions.
    Japan needs a different strategy. RPGs dominate at ~30% of JP sales versus 8–9% elsewhere. A game that performs globally may need a distinct localization approach for the Japanese market.

    Nintendo's success offers a replicable playbook: family-friendly, innovative hardware experiences with exclusive IP. Competitors looking to carve out space should identify niches outside Nintendo's primary focus rather than competing head-on.

    06 — Conclusion

    Conclusion

    This analysis of global video game sales surfaces clear patterns in industry trends, regional preferences, and publisher performance — all from a single Excel workbook. Understanding these dynamics is valuable for anyone navigating the gaming industry, from publishers planning release strategies to analysts benchmarking market performance.
